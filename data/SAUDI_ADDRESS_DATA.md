# Saudi address data attribution

The Saudi region and city names in `public/data/saudi-regions.json` and `public/data/saudi-cities.json` are adapted from the Saudi National Address Open Dataset maintained at https://github.com/yasseralsamman/saudi-national-address.

The source repository declares MIT and CC0-1.0 licenses. Geographic entries were reduced to the fields needed by the storefront (IDs and Arabic/English names) to keep the checkout payload small. The source data describes 13 administrative regions and 4,581 cities.
